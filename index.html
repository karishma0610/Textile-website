Our Story

A Family Legacy
in Every Thread

Based in Panipat (Haryana), our family has been crafting handloom carpets for over two decades. We work as a trusted third-party manufacturer, supplying finished carpets to wholesalers and upper-party dealers.

  • All carpets are 100% handwoven on traditional looms by skilled weavers
  • We supply in bulk to wholesalers, dealers, and upper-party buyers
  • Custom sizes, patterns, and colour combinations available on order
  • Competitive pricing with reliable delivery timelines
  • Quality checked at every stage before dispatch
